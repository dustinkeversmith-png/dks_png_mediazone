Prosodic Analysis

# acoustic modeling
phonemes to 
mel-spectrogram 
	audio signals frequency content over time as a 2D image
	maps frequency axis to the mel scale
	and scaling decibles
	
FFT : Time to frequency
Mel Scaling : Frequency map to mel scale via filter banks, compressing high frequencies and expanding low frequencies to mimix human hearing
Log Compression: Amplitudes are converted to decibles or log scale, stabilizing values
