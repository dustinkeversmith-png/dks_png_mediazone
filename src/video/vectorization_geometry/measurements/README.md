Spatial Dot Products via Turning Functions $\Theta(s)$A shape's boundary can be parameterized as a 1D cumulative turning angle function $\Theta(s)$ plotted against normalized arc length $s \in [0, 1]$.Comparing two shapes reduces to computing the $L_2$ distance or dot product between their 1D turning angle arrays.This is invariant to scale and rotation (via circular shift alignment) and executes in microseconds without image re-sampling.
